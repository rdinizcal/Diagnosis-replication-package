# Fixed-input licensing

`data/provenance/inputs.csv` identifies the origin and pinned commit of each
fixed requirement and trace. Inputs copied from Diagnosis commit
`05d0750395972662e9be0a1083b09a6dce7e3e65` are covered by the MIT license
bundled at `tool/LICENSE`. The running-example inputs at commit
`1f18e6bf2f808b333a37ff60908adfab96fbabfd` were also published under MIT; the
package's MIT text is at `PACKAGE-MIT.txt`.

These statements cover redistribution of the files, not ownership of the
underlying benchmark models or third-party software used to execute them.
Provenance rows remain authoritative for file-by-file source attribution.
